A desk robot that talks, moves and keeps your voice at home.

StackChan is a small robot with a screen for a face, a head that turns and nods, and a ring of lights. MaiPai for StackChan is new software for it: firmware for the robot and a small program, the host, that runs on a computer in your home.

You talk to the robot. The host sends your words to a language model you choose, on your own computer or on MaiPai Home, and the robot answers out loud. Timers, alarms, games and faces work on the robot itself, with no model at all.
