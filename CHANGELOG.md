# Changelog

All notable changes to MaiPai for StackChan are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). No version has been released yet, so everything is under Unreleased.

What is proven on a real robot and what is proven only on a computer is on [the readiness page](docs/plan/release-readiness.md). The full feature list is [What the robot can do](docs/user/features.md).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Voice: a wake word, follow-up questions, a persona text, speech speed and pitch, spoken stop, mute and volume commands, and offline phrases.
- Faces: face styles, eye colors, weather and mood overlays, a clock face, night mode, captions, and your own picture faces.
- Swipe pages and a control sheet on the robot's screen, with a lock by passcode.
- Timers, named timers, alarms and snooze.
- Reactions to pickup, shake, tilt and petting, a pet mood that follows care, dance on request, and seven games with saved best scores.
- Privacy controls: mute, camera off and full quiet by tapping the face, and cues that cannot be hidden when the robot listens or watches.
- Six languages, switched at run time: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Italian.
- Your own brain: any OpenAI-compatible model, a small model the host installs, or MaiPai Home.
- Away from home: the robot joins your own private network by itself when it cannot reach your host.
- Setup and safety: a Wi-Fi setup page with an on-screen code, pairing with a code you can revoke, reset on the robot, and signed updates with rollback.
- Optional features that are off until you turn them on: camera snapshots, remote control with a cue on the robot, and find my robot.
- A host program with a settings page, the `stackchan check` command, and several robots on one host.
- A virtual robot and a playground to try everything with no hardware.
- A web flasher (not published yet; build from source works today).
