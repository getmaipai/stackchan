# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want a private, friendly desk robot. Be the kind of person you would want to share a workbench with.

## What we expect

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their age, background or skill level.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Say what you saw and what you tried. Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Keep issues and comments on the project.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, threats or insults.
- Posting someone else's private details, or your own tokens, passwords or pairing codes.
- Sexual language or images, or unwanted attention.
- Spam and advertising.

## Report a problem

Open an issue that says only "I have a conduct report", with no detail. We will arrange a private way to receive it. There is no private email or form yet; the [pre-publish checklist](docs/dev/pre-publish-checklist.md) tracks adding one.

## Who decides

The project owner decides what happens. That can be a warning, removing a comment, or a ban from the repository. The owner reads every report and keeps the reporter's name private.
