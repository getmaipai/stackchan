# The robot never monitors in secret

Whenever the robot listens, looks or watches for you, it shows it on its own screen. There are no hidden modes. You choose the listening cues. The camera cue and the remote cue cannot be turned off.

| The robot is | You see on the robot |
|---|---|
| Listening to you | A listening light, a chime and a listening face. Each is a setting on the settings page, and the light color is yours to pick. The microphone icon shows when Status icons is on |
| Taking a picture | Pink light bars flashing twice, then steady, and a camera icon |
| Showing live view | Pink light bars, steady, and a camera icon with a LIVE dot |
| Watching a room | A camera icon and the word **Watching**, plus a microphone icon if it listens for sounds |
| Being controlled remotely | A **Remote** icon, steady, and a short tone before it speaks |

If you left the listening cues on and none of these show, none of those things are happening. The robot never streams a camera or a microphone to anything but your paired host. The watching and monitor rows in this table are planned and not built yet. The camera cue comes from the same part of the robot that turns on the camera, so it cannot be switched off separately.
Mute stops listening, and camera off stops looking. You can tap them at any time. See [Mute, camera off and full quiet](mute-and-camera-off.md).
