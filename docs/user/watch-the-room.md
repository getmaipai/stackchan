# Watch the room

> **This is a convenience, not a safety device.** Do not rely on it to watch a baby or a person who needs care. Use a purpose-made monitor for that.

<span class="planned-tag">Planned</span> Room watch and alerts are coming. Photo capture already works from the settings page.

## Safety

Room watch is not built yet. Do not use the robot to watch a pet or a room. It is not a safety device.
