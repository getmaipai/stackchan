# Watch the room

> **This is a convenience, not a safety device.** Do not rely on it to watch a baby or a person who needs care. Use a purpose-made monitor for that.

Use the robot to keep an eye on a pet or a room: take a photo, take a low-resolution look, and get an alert when it hears a loud sound or sees movement. It always shows on its own screen that it is looking or listening, and you cannot turn that off.

## What the robot can and cannot do

- It can miss a sound or a movement, lose its Wi-Fi, or run out of battery. Check on your pet in person.
- It does not know a bark from a cough. It says "loud sound".
- In a dark room the picture is dark. Use a night light.
- It does not replace care for a pet. Keep its cable out of a pet's reach, and ask your vet before leaving a pet alone for a long time.

## You can always tell

The screen shows a camera icon while the robot takes pictures and a microphone icon while it listens. The light bars show pink for the camera and green for listening. No setting hides these. If the camera is off or the robot is muted, none of this is happening. See [Use the camera](camera.md).

## What it keeps

Nothing is recorded by default. Pictures and sound stay on your host and go only to your own phone, over your home network or your private network. An alert carries a time, what it noticed and a snapshot.

## Tell the people in your home

Tell residents and visitors that the robot can look and listen. Laws about recording differ by place, and some need everyone's consent. This is not legal advice.

## Set it up

1. Put the robot where the camera can see the pet's bed or the room, a little out of reach.
2. Plug it in. A robot that runs out of battery stops watching.
3. Turn the camera on in the settings and check the picture.

## If it stops working

| What you see | What went wrong | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The screen says **Offline** | The robot lost Wi-Fi or your host | Check the room in person, then see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) |
| No alerts | The sound or motion limit is too high | Lower it, and test with a sound |
| Too many alerts | The limit is too low, or a fan is making noise | Raise it, or draw an area to ignore |
