# Response latency, false wakes and head movement, 2026-11-16 (partial)

Placement: unit firmware (patches L01, L03, L04, L05 below, tree `~/esp/stackchan-rebase/train5`, nothing committed) and bench method. Status: PARTIAL. The comparison against the original image was not run (reason below).

## What was measured and how

The unit's microphone cannot hear this Mac: the Mac Studio speakers are at volume 0 (read through CoreAudio, not changed), and a full-scale tone from the monitors raised the unit's mic peak from about 650 to 800. So no acoustic phrase test was possible. Changing a system audio level was not done.

Instead a bench-only build (`MAIPAI_SERIAL_TOOLS`) plays stored speech clips into the microphone path at real-time pace (tool `self.bench.inject`: wake phrase, a gap, one question, silence) and logs one WARN line per stage with the unit clock (`LAT <ms> <event>`). Everything after the microphone is real: wake engine, Opus, Wi-Fi, the live hub host, TTS back, decode, speaker queue. The plain image contains none of it (checked: no `self.bench`, `LAT:` strings). Helper scripts and clips: `~/esp/stackchan-rebase/latency/` (`run_turns.py`, `noise_run.py`, `recall.py`, `gen_clips.py`).

The hub host log is root-only, so host stages (end of speech, transcript, model, speech synthesis) cannot be split; they show as one number.

## Causes found

1. Wake front end ran an echo canceller on the wrong reference (legacy patch 083). `AUDIO_INPUT_REFERENCE` is true, but the second channel is the second microphone 2 cm away, not the speaker. The canceller learns the room and cancels the voice, so the wake word goes deaf after hours, and noise gets through. Fixed (L01).
2. The unit talks by itself. Before the fix, in a 600 s idle window on the owner's desk the unit had 3 wake callbacks and 6 reply starts with no phrase injected. After L01, L03, L05: 0 and 0 (one run each, room not identical; treat as a direction, not a rate).
3. Follow-up listening lasted 20 s after every reply with no echo canceller, so the unit hears its own reply tail, servo noise and the room, the hub answers it, and replies chain (observed chains of 2 to 4 replies per question). Now 8 s (L03).
4. Head movement: the unit's own idle motion was already off by default. What moves the head is the hub host calling `self.robot.set_head_angles` with nobody speaking (53 moves in 600 s before; 4 in 600 s after). Now ignored while idle unless idle movement is on (L04).
5. The unit switched itself to quiet from the face twice in about 40 minutes without a recorded touch. Open.
6. Not a cause here: persistent socket (in), TLS (plain ws), power save (performance during a turn), TCP_NODELAY is not set on the socket (a small uplink delay; not changed, it lives in a vendor component).
