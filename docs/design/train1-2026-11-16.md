# Train 1 firmware: reset, restart guard, link looks (2026-11-16)

Items 1, 4 and 5 of Train 1 in `feature-gap-2026-11-16.md`. Items 2 (wireless update) and 3 (Wi-Fi portal) belong to other lanes. Verdict per item: REBUILD (the old patches are the spec; the files they touched were rewritten or are gone). Nothing was flashed and no USB device was touched.

## Done

| Patch | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `R01-reset-on-device` | Quick settings gets Reset. It opens a screen that lists what will be erased (saved Wi-Fi, pairing, settings) and erases nothing until its button is held 3 s. Cancel or letting go early changes nothing. The clear writes a pending mark, erases every flash namespace except the mark, the head calibration (`servo`) and the radio calibration (`phy`), removes the mark last, then restarts into setup mode. A power loss inside it is finished at the next start, before any setting is read, so the unit is never half-cleared. Only the on-screen hold calls it: no tool, host command or setting. One local log line. English and Spanish text. Kconfig `MAIPAI_RESET_ON_DEVICE`, default y. | `maipai_reset_core.h` (host-tested), `maipai_reset.cc`, `stackchan_display.cc`, `hal.cpp` |
| `R02-restart-guard-and-crash-fixes` | The guard of the old 085: every self-chosen restart goes through `maipai_heal_restart`, at most 3 an hour counted in flash (own namespace `mp_heal`), the fourth is refused and reported (`heal` event, `heal_hold` in the `boot` event). The host's `reboot` command runs through it off the receiving task. The motion task restarts the unit after 30 s without a main-loop tick (never before the first tick). The restart and its flash write run on a 4 KB internal-stack task, which is the crash line of 074 (a flash write from a PSRAM-stack task aborts). `I2cDevice::ReadReg` tries 3 times, resets the bus between tries, then logs and returns 0 instead of aborting (the 0x108 abort seen once in five boots). | `maipai_heal.cc`, `hal_imu.cpp`, `application.cc`, `i2c_device.cc`, `hal.cpp` |
| `R03-link-looks` | Link screen with drawn glyphs (cycling Wi-Fi bars, slashed bars, slash with a key), Wi-Fi setup text now readable (hotspot name and address on the screen, also after the boot), a corner strip after the first face (3 s: bars cycle; 45 s: slashed bars and "Offline" with the amber ring), and "Waiting for Home to approve me" when Home closes the socket before its hello twice in a row (full screen before the first face, strip and cyan ring after it, `ws_refused` event, cleared by the first hello). Shapes only: no font, no image, no mint skin part. The glyphs and strip exist only while a look shows. | `stackchan_display.cc`, `stackchan.cc`, `hal_bridge.h`, `websocket_protocol.cc` |

All three are on `firmware/patches/ORDER` after `160-first-boot-rollback`. Host test: `host/tests/firmware/reset_core_test.c` through `host/tests/test_stackchan_reset_core.py` (3 passed).

## Skipped, with the reason

- 074 boot defaults (3600 s idle shutdown, boot into the agent): product values; boot already starts the agent in this base. 074 `keep_awake` tool: already present.
- 069 and 084 stall record and core-dump report: diagnostic only, on the drop list.
- 085 "deaf" restart reason: needs 086 (wake self-test), which is not built.
- 112 muted "offline" face palette and eyes 5 px lower: the face colour belongs to the saved face config (F01); a firmware-only transient palette risks being saved. The strip, the words and the ring already say offline.
- 112 "Can't reach Home" riding on the strip: the existing message stays where it is.
- 111 to 113 Spanish text: the link text is English only, like the boot text it replaces. R01 is English and Spanish.
- R01 Spanish has no accents (the built-in font has none and no font may be added).
