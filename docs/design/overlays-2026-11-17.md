# Overlay readability pass, 2026-11-17 (patches O01 and O02)

Owner finding: at arm's length the overlays were too small and thin (zzz were outlines, rain was a 1 px line, snow a 2 px dot). This note is
the design record: the size rules, the layers, how the text zones and the face stay clear, the named combinations and the cost.

## Layers, order and what is never covered

- Three layers: 0 back (weather), 1 middle (events), 2 front (only combinations). A single overlay fills its own layer (weather or event);
  a combination fills the layers it needs and replaces everything; a single overlay started during a combination ends the combination
  (no half pictures). Layers draw front to back.
- Every overlay is behind the face. A pixel is painted only when it is background (luma at most 10 of 31), and the first paint of a pixel
  wins, so a front cloud covers a back cloud and a fill covers its outline with no per-shape ordering code.
- The eyes and the mouth of the style on screen are protected ellipses (`mc_protect`), because a pupil and an open mouth are as dark as
  the background. Positions come from the style table (screen y 106 is the middle of the face zone).
- The text zones are left alone while they show: the banner row (y 0 to 27) when a message, the chip or any strip glyph is on
  (`maipai_banner_zone_busy`), the subtitle strip (y 184 to 239) while subtitles show. The text layers draw above the overlays anyway; the
  rule keeps the overlay out from under them. When a zone appears or goes away its rows are invalidated once.
- The eye halo (glow) and the eye protection both take the eye center from `ml_eye_center_y` in the shared look table (face zone middle,
  y 106, plus the style's eye offset). The glow was placed from y 120, the middle of the screen, which N04 stopped using, so it sat 14 px
  low; `look_core_test.c` now checks that the center follows the table for every style (patch O02).
- The thunder flash is a wash over every pixel that is not a bright face part (the clouds light up with the sky), skipped in the protected
  ellipses and the text zones.

## Performance contract

- State: three layers of 40 particles of 14 bytes and 40 dirty rectangles are 2.76 KB, allocated once in PSRAM (patch TC03 rule); internal
  RAM adds two booleans. No heap use after that. The stack in the flush path holds one canvas (about 90 bytes).
- Falling things move every second tick, twice as far (15 pictures a second): the flushed pixels halve. Clouds move two pixels at a time,
  a zzz changes every third tick, fireworks sparks every second tick.
- Each tick marks only the boxes of things that changed; `overlay_core_test.c` compares the panel after partial flushes with a full
  redraw for every overlay and combination, at light and heavy, and again at the end of a run (nothing is left behind).
- The governor (TA05, reworked in O03, see below) sheds glow and scanlines first, then halves the particles, then goes to 30 percent
  (`mo_alive_cap`; a burst is shortened so one fits; the back layer of clouds is not drawn). A running overlay is never turned off except in an emergency.
- Modeled cost, 30 simulated seconds (`host/tests/firmware/fx_cost.c`; the constants are stated in the file and the unit numbers are
  measured after the flash): flushed pixels per tick at normal, share of the panel's SPI time at normal and at heavy, the same at heavy

## How to check

1. `scripts/fw-face-render/overlays_render.sh` draws every overlay and combination with the real face and the banner row: a sheet of six moments
   on Big eyes (`NAME.png`), the five styles at phone-photo size, 300 px wide (`NAME_phone.png`) and the text-zone pictures (`NAME_zones.png`).
2. `host/tests/firmware/overlay_core_test.c` (sizes, zones, protection, combinations, caps, dirty rectangles, zzz placement) and `fx_cost.c`.
3. `host/tests/test_overlay_preview_parity.py`: the settings page picker and preview draw from `overlay_table.json`, which must equal the core's table.
4. After a flash: `self.overlay.set` for each of the 26 names, a banner message and a spoken reply over each, `scripts/fw-serial.py snap`, and the
   heap and governor lines of `self.overlay.status`.

## The governor fix (patch O03, from integration 2)

On the unit the governor sat at its flat look 68 percent of a 12 minute run: at the flat level it restored one step, the overlays came back
with one full-screen redraw of 60 to 120 ms, and that one slow frame shed it straight back. Stormy, party, celebrate, rain and the sleeping
zzz showed nothing in the snapshots. The rules now:

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| A slow frame | over 45 ms |
| Shed one level | at least 3 slow frames among the last 10 (6 of 10 during the grace period) |
| Grace period | 1.5 s after an overlay starts, the overlays come back, or a level is restored |
| Restore one level | 2 s with no slow frame (an idle second counts as fast) |
| Ladder | glow off, scanlines off, particles 50 percent, particles 30 percent (the floor), flat look |
| Floor | while any overlay runs, the ladder stops at 30 percent; only an emergency goes on to flat |
| Emergency | the 99th percentile of frame time over 100 ms for 5 seconds in a row; it clears at the next restore |
| Coming back | no full redraw: `mo_unhide_step` marks about 6000 pixels of boxes a tick (fixed areas, then particle boxes), and the particles return in two steps (30, 50, then 100 percent) |

`fx_core_test.c` replays frame traces and shows no oscillation: calm; sustained 60 ms frames (the ladder in order, flat at the end with no overlay);
a lone 120 ms frame at an unhide (nothing sheds); the grace period; restores every 2 s; sustained 70 ms frames with an overlay (stops at 30 percent
with exactly 4 sheds and no restores, then climbs back with no further shed); the emergency (flat only after 5 s of 130 ms frames); and the unit's own
12 minute trace (20 ms frames with a 100 ms redraw after every restore: zero sheds, never flat, full for over 95 percent of the run).
`overlay_core_test.c` checks that the staged unhide covers every pixel of the full-extent boxes and that no step is a full-screen redraw.
The host renderer pictures are byte for byte the same (85 files compared).
