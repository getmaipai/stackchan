## How a sound starts with its picture

The Color Memory fix (UI03) holds a tone until the first display strip that carries its tile. The same rule now covers every game: a rule names the region its picture changes in (the `sync` action) and the sound waits for the first strip that overlaps that region, or 100 ms on a dark screen. A rule with no `sync` plays at once. Whack-a-mole syncs a hit and an escape to the mole's cell and a miss to the tapped cell, Tic-Tac-Toe a mark to its cell, Do It a cue to the cue box, Rock Paper Scissors a result to the tapped button.

## Inputs from the unit's own sensors

The imu task and the head touch task run on PSRAM stacks, so they never touch flash or NVS (`scripts/psram-stack-lint.py` passes, 0 unreviewed paths). They call three functions (`maipai_games_post_input`, `maipai_games_imu_feed`, `maipai_games_shake`) that use atomics only: no lock, no allocation, no flash. The game's 30 ms tick reads them.

- Shake: the existing motion detector (threshold 9 m/s^2 over 100 ms) posts one event.
- Pet: a press on the head strip posts one event.
- Tilt: a new detector reads the accelerometer every 100 ms. The pose the unit rests in when a game starts is its level; a tilt is the pull leaving it by about 22 degrees on one axis for two samples, and it must come back within 0.15 g before the next one counts. Nothing counts while the pull's length is not one g (the unit is moving) or right after a shake. Directions follow the host's orientation table (the unit's right ear down reads +x, face down reads -z).
- Swipes: a finger moving left or right inside the play area, judged by the same rule as the page swipes (36 px, 900 ms, 60 px/s). Swipes that start in the side edges stay the volume's.

## UI15 and the final merged image (2026-11-17)

`UI15` adds two data-pack games, Magic 8-Ball (`eightball`) and Dice and Coin (`dice`), on small engine additions: a die shape and a coin shape, a ring-light action, a celebrate action, four more sound clips (rattle, thud, mystic hum, coin ring), 32 texts and 64 shapes per pack. Voice: "play magic 8 ball", "ask the magic 8 ball", "roll a dice", "flip a coin", "play dice" and Spanish forms start the game by the local command path (the host answers before the model); "flip a coin" starts Dice and Coin, where the Coin tile is one tap away.

- **Gentle shake.** The games get every 20 ms accelerometer sample and use their own detector: strokes of 2.2 m/s^2 (0.22 g) on the moving axis, three within 0.9 s, 0.8 s quiet time afterwards. A wobble of 3 cm at 3 Hz (about 10 m/s^2), 1.5 cm at 3 Hz (about 5) and 1 cm at 2.5 Hz (about 3) all count, and a 5 m/s^2 wobble counts in under half a second. A tiny tremor (1 m/s^2), a lone bump, a slow roll and standing still do not. At 20 m/s^2 (2 g) or more the shake is too hard: it posts `MG_EV_EASY`, the game answers "Easy!" and gives no points. Hints and tiles say "Gentle shake" / "Agita suave" (core test `test_gentle_shake`).
- **Celebrations.** A won Tic-Tac-Toe game, a won Rock Paper Scissors round (a won match gets the big one, `GM04`) and every new best score (place 0 of the saved list, never a loss) start the overlay engine: confetti for a win, confetti and fireworks for 4 s for a new best, with the happy face, through `maipai_fx_celebrate` (the governor sheds it, the banner row and subtitle strip stay clear, and it does nothing when overlays are off). The overlay engine paints over the finished frame, so a game cannot put it behind its text; the confetti is sparse and the result lines stay readable (unit snapshot `19_ttt_win_confetti.png`).
- **Motion.** Arithmetic on existing objects, no heap: the mole pops with an ease-out overshoot and is squashed when struck, with stars circling; the dice hop and bounce in turn, the coin flips edge-on, bounces and glints; the eight ball wobbles on a damped spring and its window fades in and floats; the Rock Paper Scissors fist bumps with the beat and both hands pop at the reveal (`GM05`); Tic-Tac-Toe marks draw themselves in; Do It's icon pops when it is shown. Not done: dice rotation (LVGL objects do not rotate cheaply), the Tic-Tac-Toe winning-line sweep, the Do It timer ring and score count-up.
- **Frame gaps** (bench build, `self.game.bench_gaps`, gaps between display strips while a game moves, no tool call during the window): Magic 8-Ball p50 5 ms, p99 45 to 70 ms over three runs (worst 160 to 255); Dice p50 5 ms, p99 55 to 140 ms (worst 200 to 255). A game standing still showed p99 up to 255 ms in the same measurements, so some of the stalls are the rest of the firmware (the face underneath, Wi-Fi and the roaming code), not the motion; the dice amplitudes were reduced and the clips are rendered at the game's start, which did not change the numbers.
