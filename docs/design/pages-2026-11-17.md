# Swipe gestures, the control sheet and swipe pages, 2026-11-17 (patches P10 to P13)

Owner idea (2026-11-16, brief `.runs/brief-firmware-next.md`): the face is home. A swipe up from the bottom opens a control sheet; a swipe
left or right moves through pages the owner chose. Built in `~/esp/stackchan-rebase/pages` (the final3 tree plus O03, FI06 and K10 to K13),
patches appended to `ORDER` after K13. No release image was flashed. One bench window was taken (about 3 minutes, bench image only, the plain image put back and its read-back hash checked): what
passed and what did not is under "The bench window".

## What the owner gets

| Swipe | Meaning |
|---|---|
| up, begun in the bottom band (y 160 to 239) | opens the control sheet (the lower 60 percent of the screen at most, never the banner row) |
| down, begun anywhere | the stop swipe (stays): stops speaking, ends a game. Over the sheet it closes it; over a page it leaves it |
| left or right, begun at x 20 to 299 | moves through the pages; the finger moving right reaches the page on the left of the face |
| left or right, begun in a side edge (x under 20 or 300 and over) | never a page change: the old edge meaning, volume minus or plus 10 |
| tap above the open sheet | closes it |

A swipe is at least 36 px, mostly along one axis (1.5 to 1), within 900 ms and not slower than 60 px a second. A slow drag (a slider, a resting
finger) is never one. A touch that ended a ringing timer, woke a dimmed screen, began while quick settings was open, or began during a game
(a game keeps its touches; only the stop swipe and the edge swipes work) changes no page.

Back to the face: the wake word and the listening status, speech, a touch on the head, a ringing timer; a page after 20 s without a touch
(5 to 300), the sheet after 6 s (5 to 15), counted from the last touch and stopped while a finger is down. A head event is dropped while a
finger is on the screen, so a head touch cannot pull a page out from under a finger. A head swipe is never a page change.

Defaults: the clock on the left of the face, the games on the right, nothing else until chosen. Up to three pages on each side, each page once.

## The pages (only the one on screen exists)

Zones: banner y 0 to 28 and subtitles y 184 to 240 stay as they are; a page lives in y 28 to 184. Fonts: the build's 20 px Latin text font and
Montserrat 14, 16, 20 and 24. No new font, no picture asset: every shape is drawn.

| Page | Content | Quiet and night |
|---|---|---|
| Clock | seven-bar digits (44 by 87) or a seconds ring with smaller digits, date line, AM or PM; a dash until the clock is set; works with no host | dimmed to 35 percent in the night band |
| Timers | up to three running timers (label, time left, cancel) and three quick starts (1, 5, 10 min) | none |
| Games | up to three games with their best score; a row is a play button | a started game closes the page |
| Quick actions | 4 to 6 big buttons from find, beep, dance, game, hello | in full quiet find, beep, dance and hello are dim and a tap says why; under a mute find and beep are dim |
| Pictures | the picture face pack's frames, one at a time, every 8 s, 192 by 144 on a 150 KB PSRAM buffer that exists only while the page shows | holds still at night and in full quiet |
| Dashboard | up to six host-fed tiles: icon (ten drawn icons), label (12), value (10); the core ships no data source | older than an hour: dim, and the age is written; at night dimmed |

The control sheet: a status line (Wi-Fi bars, battery percent, private network, settings address), two sliders (volume 20 to 100, brightness
10 to 100), buttons of 72 by 38 for mute, quiet, listening light, find, timers and games with on and off written out (never color alone), and
**More**, which opens the old quick settings panel (saved Wi-Fi, reset, the version and network screens). Items, order and what is shown
come from the saved choice. Overlays (weather effects) pause while a page or the sheet is up, like under the clock face.

Not built: Weather and Music mood as page types of their own (a weather tile fits the dashboard; music mood needs its own design). The brief's
scope for this pass named the six above.

## Settings page

Behavior, then **Swipe pages**: a preview of the screen (face, each page in the strip, the sheet), two lists (left and right, nearest the face
first) with drag, up, down and remove, closing times, quick actions (4 to 6) and sheet items, **Show on the robot** and **Send sample tiles**. The unit tools
are feature-detected: a unit without them keeps the choice, shows "Coming with the next unit update" and gets it on connect. The preview draws from
`pages_table.json`; `pgLayout` in the page script is the core's arithmetic and a test runs both.

## Cost and performance contract

- Flash: `maipai_pages.cc.obj` 19.6 KB and `maipai_pages_unit.cc.obj` 23.6 KB of text and read-only data (bench build objects), plus small hooks. The bench image grew by
  44,656 bytes against the final3 bench image (4,043,920 to 4,088,576) and the plain image by 42,640 (3,445,184 to 3,487,824); both differences also carry O03, FI06 and K10 to K13. The app partition has 21 percent (bench) and 33 percent (plain) free.
- Internal RAM at rest: 112 bytes (pages) and about 400 bytes (the unit host: tiles, a view copy, a mutex, a few flags), measured from the object files: about 510 bytes, under the 2 KB limit. No timer, no LVGL
  object, no picture buffer, no task at rest. On the unit: 79,871 B internal free before any page and 79,603 B after all of them were shown and closed.
- While something shows: the 250 ms LVGL timer, the page's objects (clock 40, timers 25, games 21, quick actions 19, pictures 9, dashboard 53, sheet 24; counted
  in the host renderer, the banner and subtitles hold none at rest; on the unit the dashboard page took about 13 KB of internal RAM while it was up) and, on the Pictures page, 153,600 bytes of PSRAM. Everything is freed when it closes.
- The Pictures page makes one PSRAM-stack task (`frame_task`, 20 KB in PSRAM, about 400 bytes of internal RAM for its control block) the first time a frame is asked for and keeps it
  (a task with a PSRAM stack cannot end itself). It never touches flash or settings; reviewed in `test_firmware_trackc_patches.py` and `scripts/psram-stack-lint.py` (0 unreviewed paths).
- Draw cost, host renderer with dirty-area flushing (`PERF=1`), pixels flushed per 33 ms frame averaged over 3 s: face alone 3,584; clock 4,083; timers 3,628; games 3,244;
  quick actions 1,818; dashboard 1,706; pictures 2,441; sheet 2,349. A page costs about what the face costs; the face keeps animating under it. A bug found by this
  measure and fixed: setting a style on a toggle every refresh repainted the sheet (10,557 px a frame); a style is now set only when the state changes.
- The pages never hold the LVGL lock longer than one page build (about 20 to 55 objects).

## The bench window (one, about 3 minutes, 2026-11-17)

Bench image from the work tree (the `usb-queue.txt` line `pages-p13`, then `usb-switch.lock`), driver `pages/winp.py`, logs and 14 snapshots in `~/esp/stackchan-rebase/pages/logs/winp/`
(`pics/*.png`, `ser/fw-serial.log`, `winp.log`). At the end the plain image listed as current in `integration2-2026-11-17.md` (final3 plain, sha256 `c1583752...`, 3,445,184 bytes) went back; the device
hash was verified and the read-back hash equals it. 25 of 33 checks passed.

Passed on the real unit:

- `self.pages.status` (version 1), `self.pages.get` (defaults), `self.pages.set` (new strip read back, a repeated page refused with its sentence), `self.page.show` for all six pages, the sheet and an unknown name (refused with its sentence), `self.page.tiles` (six accepted, twelve refused).
- Every page and the sheet built and drew on the real display: the snapshots show the clock, timers, quick actions, dashboard and the sheet as the PC renderer drew them (dimmed, because the unit's clock was in the night band).
- A swipe left from the face reached the games page and a swipe down left it; a slow drag, a tiny move and the edge swipes changed no page.
- A page returned to the face by itself after 21.3 s (the setting is 20).
- Memory (the unit's own `SystemInfo` lines): 79,871 B internal free at rest; the lowest while pages and the sheet were up was 66,887 B (about 13 KB for the busiest page, the dashboard with 53 objects); 79,603 B after they closed
  (268 B kept: the picture task's control block and flash-cache residue); `int_big` stayed 31,744; `psram_min` 7,444,672 (the bench snapshots hold two 150 KB buffers); the LVGL task's lowest free stack was 2,816 B (the floor is 1,024, FI04 asked for 2,048).

Did not pass, and why:

- Six synthetic swipes (right to the clock, a second right, the sheet by a swipe up, the sheet closing, two swipes in the changed strip) and the check that the sheet closes by itself (it needed the sheet opened by a swipe).
  Cause, found in the log and the code: the bench finger wrote the touch point, and the panel's own driver writes "no finger" over it every few milliseconds, so most synthetic swipes were lifted at once; the ones that passed got through between two
  driver writes. It is a flaw of the test finger, not of the pages. Fixed in patch P13: `hal_bridge::touch_inject` (bench builds only) makes `set_touch_point` ignore the driver while a bench finger is down. The fixed bench image builds (4,088,816 bytes)
  but was not flashed: the one window was used. The first thing to run at the final integration is `python3 pages/winp.py OUTDIR` with the serial daemon up; it repeats every swipe check.
- The memory check by tool: this bench image has no `self.game.status`. The memory numbers above come from the unit's own log lines instead.

Not checked on a unit yet: the sheet and a swipe up from the bottom, the wake word and a head touch from a page, the Pictures page with a pack held, the quick actions pressed, the sheet's sliders and buttons (all in the list below).

## Decisions taken without the owner

- The finger moving right reaches the page on the left of the face (the way a phone home screen works). The settings page says so beside each list.
- The sheet's **More** opens the old quick settings panel (Wi-Fi networks, reset, version and network screens stay where the user docs say) instead of a second copy of them.
  The cost is one more tap on the path to the network test; the user docs say so.
- The sheet may cover up to the lower 60 percent of the screen (the brief said about the lower half): tiles are 72 by 38 px so they are 4.8 mm or more on the 2 inch screen.
- The idle time for a page has no "never" value; 300 s is the longest.
- Tiles pushed by the host live in RAM and are saved to flash at most once in ten minutes, so a host that pushes every minute does not wear the flash.
