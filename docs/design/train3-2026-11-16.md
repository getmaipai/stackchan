### What blocks more checks
- The unit's saved state is hard muted. That is why sounds, the clock face and the wake word could not be checked. Unmuting turns the microphone on, so it was not done; it is the owner's call.
- No one looked at the screen and no camera watched it, so "the face drew a sad or happy look" and "the timer ring showed" are not observed. The replies and log lines are.
- A head pet raising care was not tried (needs a touch). That the saved record survives a reset is observed (the record written after the first Train 3 flash was loaded after the next reset), but a changed care value surviving is not.
- Sound policy: after my calls the readback equals the readback before them (enabled 0, night_ok 0, muted 1, quiet 1260 to 420), but one value I sent (1320) did not come back, so something rewrote the policy in between. The older hub host is the likely cause and that was not observed. A partial `set_sound_policy` call also resets the fields it leaves out.
- Old hub host still calls `self.music.set` (45) and `self.screen.set_face_background` (65) in 11 minutes; both are unknown on the unit (a host change, not a firmware one).
