# Train 6: offline phrases, music dance and dance extras, 2026-11-16

Placement: unit firmware (patches `V02`, `V03`, `V04`, native lane) and one host list (`companion.py`). Tree: `~/esp/stackchan-rebase/train6` (a copy of the Train 4 tree), ESP-IDF 6.1.1. Nothing is committed or pushed. The patches, host tests and this note are uncommitted files in this repo.

Where it is: patches are files in the repo working tree only (not on GitHub `main`, in no release, not on the live hub). The firmware is flashed on the owned unit on the USB cable: the plain Train 6 image (details below). Replaying `V02`, `V03`, `V04` with `git apply` onto a copy of the Train 4 tree gives a tree byte-identical to the one built (`diff -r` clean).

| 2 Music dance | `V03` | `MAIPAI_MUSIC_DANCE` (default y, off at run time) | Legacy patches 100, 101, 121, 124, 126 to 128, 130, 131 rebuilt as one change. A tap in the audio input task (one atomic read per chunk while off), a front end of four band-pass filters, a 4 KB PSRAM worker task with about 3 KB of PSRAM history, tempo, flatness, speech-like modulation and pauses once a second. Home reachable: the unit sends `music_feat` numbers and the host decides (as before). Home unreachable: the same C judge the host tests run (nod with a happy face, head bob on the beat, the dance 15 s later that sways on the song's beat, a slow mood between blocks, pause after 3 s without music, end after 60 s). | 3 Dance extras | `V04` | `MAIPAI_DANCE_EXTRAS` (default y) | Six named dances as data (`maipai_dances_core.h`): sway, nod_yes, shake_no, wiggle, bow, groove. Each is a checked row (turn at most 60 degrees, tilt -30 to +25, speed 50 to 1000, step 100 ms to 2 s, 8 s in all, centred at both ends) that becomes the built-in keyframe type only while it plays (about 1 KB of heap). `self.robot.dance` takes the new names, `self.robot.dance_list` names all ten. `ORDER` ends `V02`, `V03`, `V04`. Each patch has `# Intent:`, `# Placement:`, `# Upstream:`, `# Check after a flash:` and `# Kconfig:` lines.

## Decisions

- Wake and stop checks (item 1 condition). The unit is hard muted in its saved state (`self.privacy.get` says muted), so the wake engine is off and nothing can be spoken to it; the stop word needs a person. Train 3 skipped patch 118 for the same reason. This patch was built anyway because it cannot touch either check: the only call it changes is the tone inside `ShowCantReachHome`, which runs after the wake word has already fired and the audio channel failed to open. No audio input, wake, stop-word or audio task code is in `V02`. The wake and stop checks were not run and are listed under Not verified.
- Offline voice. The sixteen clips are the household's default synthetic voice (the manifest says so: not a recording of a person), not a recording of the owner. Replacing them is a swap of the sixteen files and a rebuild, no code. Low battery, wake acknowledgement and timer done have no clip (the existing sets are about the link); they keep their tones.
- `MAIPAI_MUSIC_DANCE` default. Measured 2.3 KB of steady free internal RAM for all three patches together (table below), under the 4 KB rule, so it ships default y. At run time it is off until `self.music.set` turns it on (the host does this when its dance setting is on), and while off the audio path pays one atomic read per chunk. Nothing is read while the unit is muted or in full quiet: `ReadAudioData` returns before the tap (patch 107).

## A bug the unit found

The first bench build with the music worker crashed in a boot loop: the worker (stack in PSRAM) called `maipai_muted()`, whose first call reads the saved privacy choice from flash, and a flash read on a PSRAM stack fails the assert in `spi_flash_disable_interrupts_caches_and_other_cpu`. The fix reads it once in `maipai_music_init()` on the internal stack before the worker starts; a host test (`test_the_music_worker_never_reads_flash_from_its_psram_stack`) now fails if the worker or the order changes. The earlier builds had passed only by timing.

### What was observed over the serial tool path

Every reply read.

- Offline phrases: `self.audio.sound_info` says `clips_present` 16. `self.audio.offline_phrase` (dry) for no_wifi, no_host, refused, connecting and back each answers `kind clip`, the group, variant 1 and the clip's byte count (4030, 5248, 4236, 5264, 2264), equal to the manifest, so all five groups' clips are in flash and read back intact; an unknown group is refused with a plain error. A non-dry call on the real unit answers `kind none` (muted, as it must). With the saved sounds-off choice on this unit (`enabled 0`) the real path would give the tone only, by design.
- Music dance, with a synthetic click track (a throwaway tool fed 24 kHz stereo into the tap, mute check bypassed in that build only, link treated as down): tempo 120 and 100 BPM were measured exactly with beat periods 500 and 600 ms; the session went idle, nod, dance (read from `self.music.get_status`) about 4 and 20 seconds in; switching the link to up stopped the dance; switching it down started a new session; `self.music.set false` stopped it; after the click track ended the state went to paused and then idle. The dance counter, queue drops (0) and suppressed chunks (0) read back.
- Dance extras: `self.robot.dance_list` names four built-in and six extras with their lengths (6700, 2100, 1900, 1460, 2800, 3500 ms). Each of the six answered true and ran; `self.robot.stop_dance` answered true; `mars` answered false. The head angles were read after a stop (the head stays where it was stopped, as for the built-ins).
- Face and decorator tools: `self.face.get_config` reports the feature words including `packs` and `decor`; `self.face.decor_list` lists the nine; `self.face.decorate sweat` is refused with "a mute, vision-off or quiet look or a ringing timer is showing" (muted unit, as designed); an unknown name gets a plain error.
- Reset screen and quick settings (a throwaway build opened them without a finger): the quick settings opened and closed five times and the reset screen opened and closed once with no fault; the panel's objects exist only while open (free internal RAM 47.1 KB closed after, about 2.4 KB less with the reset screen open, back when closed); the volume slider's event changed the codec volume (100 to 35, read back) and the brightness slider's event changed the backlight (100 to 60, read back on the next call); both were put back to 100 and read back. The More page opened with no fault.

## Not verified

- Hearing anything: the unit is muted, so no clip was played aloud and the wake word and the stop word were not exercised. The clip playing path (queueing a 2 to 5 KB clip into the decoder) and its brief heap use were not measured; the clips are small and the gate has 24 KB between the lowest reading and the floor.
- Real music: only a synthetic click track went through the front end. Real songs, the tempo estimate on real audio, and a person judging that the head sways on the beat were not tried; the host tests run the C front end on synthetic signals only. Mood faces and the head motion were not seen (nobody watched the unit and no camera did); the face and motion code ran without fault.
- The unit's own speech and chimes not starting a nod: the suppress flag is code (own sound, listening, 1.5 s after) and was not exercised with sound.
- Portal start and the setup page served from the unit: not run. The portal starts only when the unit has no saved network, and forgetting the household network cannot be undone without the Wi-Fi password, which is not mine to type. The page and routes are tested on a PC only (`portal-status-2026-11-16.md`).
- Screen swipes and the quick-settings sliders by finger: the handlers' effects were driven by a throwaway tool; a finger gesture was not made. The look of the screens was not seen.
- A flash storage fault making the travel save fail, and the other Train 4 items listed there, are unchanged.
- Twice in this session (both the first time the cable was opened after a flash) the first boot stopped at an assert in the stock camera driver start (`dvp_vsync_isr` in `esp_video`, a vertical-sync interrupt before its queue exists) and rebooted into a normal boot. It happens in board init before any Train 6 code runs; not investigated, and it is a stock-code race a flash-then-open sequence seems to trigger.
