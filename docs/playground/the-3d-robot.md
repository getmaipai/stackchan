# The 3D robot

The 3D view shows the StackChan body. What you can do depends on the build of the viewer; the controls panel lists what is on in yours.

- **Turn it.** Drag to orbit. Scroll to zoom.
- **Move the head.** The head follows the robot's commands within its limits. Pitch and yaw have stops, as the real servos do.
- **Look inside.** A switch hides the shell so you can see the servos, the battery and the boards.
- **Lights.** The twelve lights of the ring show the colors the real ring would show.
- **Power and ports.** Set the battery level, plug a cable into the side or back USB port, or carry the robot. The robot's own rules answer: on a cable the head moves less, and when it is carried or on its side the head holds still.

The model was matched by eye against photos of a real unit. Sizes are close, not measured. See `docs/dev/virtual-unit-3d.md` for how the model file is made.
